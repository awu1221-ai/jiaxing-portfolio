About Me

I’m Jiaxing, a Computer Science student driven by a genuine curiosity about how technology works and how it can be used to build things that matter. My journey into programming started with a fascination for problem-solving — taking a complex challenge and breaking it down into clear, logical steps until a solution emerges. That same mindset is what draws me to software engineering at scale: understanding distributed systems, writing efficient algorithms, and designing architectures that can handle real-world demand.

Through my coursework and self-directed learning, I’ve built a strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, and web development. I enjoy working across the stack — from crafting clean, responsive frontends to building reliable backend services. I’m actively working on personal projects to deepen my skills and preparing for software engineering roles at leading technology companies where I can contribute to meaningful products and learn from talented teams.

Beyond the screen, I have a creative side that keeps me grounded: I love making handmade crafts. Whether it’s working with paper, wood, fabric, or other materials, there’s something deeply satisfying about shaping a physical object with your own hands. Craft-making has taught me patience, attention to detail, and the value of iterative refinement — qualities that carry directly into how I approach code. I believe the best engineers share a maker’s mindset: they care about the finish, the structure, and the experience, not just whether something compiles.

I’m also interested in open-source development, competitive programming, and understanding how high-performing engineering teams collaborate and ship reliable software. I see every project as a chance to learn something new and push my abilities further.
